Use this as a reference before sales calls. Read component by component. Let each one land before moving to the next.

## The Stack

**"Here's everything you get with the Jobs Booked Pro system:"**

### Component 1: High-Converting HVAC Website -- $3,500
Custom-built website designed specifically for HVAC companies. Mobile-first (because 70%+ of your customers search from their phone). Click-to-call on every page. Service area pages. Trust badges. Before/after galleries. Built to turn visitors into booked jobs.

### Component 2: AI Voice Agent -- $3,000 setup + $300/month
"Never Miss a Call Again." An AI receptionist that answers every call your team misses -- nights, weekends, lunch breaks. Takes the caller's info, qualifies the job, and books it on your calendar. Your competitor gets the job when you don't pick up. This fixes that.

### Component 3: CRM + Lead Pipeline Setup -- $2,500
Done-for-you CRM so every lead is tracked from first call to completed job. No more sticky notes. No more "I forgot to follow up." You see exactly where every prospect is and what needs to happen next.

### Component 4: Automated Nurture Sequences -- $2,000
Email + SMS sequences that follow up with every lead automatically. Quote sent but no response? The system follows up at day 1, day 3, day 7. Seasonal maintenance reminders to past customers. You stop chasing -- the system chases for you.

### Component 5: Google Business Profile Optimization -- $1,500
Full optimization of your Google Business Profile so you show up in the map pack when someone searches "HVAC near me." Photos, categories, service descriptions, review strategy -- all dialed in.

### Component 6: 90-Day SEO Content Plan -- $1,500
A custom content roadmap targeting the exact searches your customers make -- "AC repair [your city]," "furnace not blowing hot air," "HVAC emergency service." You'll know exactly what to post and why.

### BONUS: Online Booking Integration -- $1,000
Customers book appointments directly from your website -- no phone call needed. Syncs with your calendar. Sends confirmation texts automatically.

### BONUS: Review Generation System -- $750
Automated text/email after every completed job asking for a Google review. More reviews = higher rankings = more calls.

## Objection Handling

**"Too expensive"**
"One HVAC job averages $3,000 to $8,000. This system pays for itself with the first two jobs it books. Everything after that is profit."

**"I already have a website"**
"You have a website. But is it booking jobs? How many calls did it generate last month? The Jobs Booked system isn't a website -- it's a revenue engine with a website attached."

**"I need to think about it"**
"Absolutely. What specific concerns do you want to think through? I might be able to address them right now."

**Push back on price**
Don't cut. Add. "I'll throw in a competitor analysis showing exactly what the top 3 HVAC companies in your area are doing online, and where you can beat them. That's normally a separate engagement."
