## NOTES FOR BUILD

- Mobile-first. Everything thumb-friendly.
- Dark, premium feel. Not the typical blue-and-white HVAC look. Stand out.
- Value stack section should animate in one component at a time on scroll.
- Pricing table should feel clean and confident, not salesy.
- Guarantee section needs visual weight -- make it feel like a promise, not a footnote.
- Social proof / testimonials section can be added once first clients are live.
- No stock photos of generic HVAC techs. Use icons or abstract visuals until real photos exist.
