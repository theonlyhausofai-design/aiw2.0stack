# HVAC Conversion Rules Contract

## Principle

Every element on the page exists to move the homeowner from "I need help" to "I just called." Remove anything that does not serve that path.

## Above the Fold (Hero Section)

1. Split-screen hero. Image on one side (technician or family), headline and CTA on the other. Never a full-bleed image with tiny overlaid text.
2. Trust strip directly below the hero or embedded in it. Four claims max: Licensed and Insured, NATE Certified, Same-Day Service, Satisfaction Guaranteed.
3. Primary CTA visible without scrolling on both desktop and mobile. "Schedule Service Now" button plus a visible phone number.
4. No sliders. No carousels in the hero. One message, one image, one CTA.

## Click-to-Call

5. Phone number in the top-right of the nav on desktop. Sticky.
6. On mobile, a persistent sticky bottom bar with "Tap to Call Now" that never scrolls away.
7. Every page ends with a phone CTA in the footer banner.
8. Phone number is a tel: link everywhere. No exceptions.

## Trust Placement

9. Trust badges (NATE, BBB, manufacturer logos) appear in a horizontal strip within the first viewport on the homepage.
10. Service pages repeat the trust strip below the service hero.
11. Reviews carousel appears on every page except Contact. Minimum 3 reviews visible at once on desktop, 1 on mobile with swipe.

## Process Section

12. Three-step process appears on the homepage and every service page. Call Us, We Diagnose, We Fix It. Numbered, with icons. Keeps things simple and predictable for the homeowner.

## Financing Prominence

13. If the client offers financing, it gets its own nav item or a visible badge near every installation-related CTA. "Monthly payments as low as $X" near the CTA button on installation pages.

## Forms

14. Short forms only. Name, phone, service needed, preferred time. Four fields max on the initial form. No address, no email required for first contact.
15. Form header matches copy-locks.json. "Get Fast HVAC Service."
16. Submit button is action-oriented: "Request Service" not "Submit."
17. Privacy line below the button. "Your information is secure and never shared."

## Mobile-Specific Rules

18. Sticky bottom CTA bar on all pages. Call button on left, form link on right. Always visible.
19. No horizontal scrolling. No elements that break the viewport.
20. Tap targets minimum 44x44px. No tiny links.
21. Forms stack single-column on mobile. No side-by-side fields.

## Review Carousel

22. Show star rating, reviewer name (first name + last initial), and the review text. Source badge (Google, Yelp) for credibility.
23. Auto-advance disabled. User swipes or clicks only.

## Service Page CRO Pattern

Each service page follows this structure top to bottom:

1. Service hero with headline, subtext, and primary CTA
2. Trust strip
3. Problem/symptom list (what the homeowner is experiencing)
4. Process steps
5. Pricing transparency section (range, estimate, or "call for quote" with context)
6. Filtered reviews for that service
7. FAQ accordion (3-5 questions specific to that service)
8. Final CTA banner with phone number and form link

## Exit Intent

24. No exit-intent popups. They damage trust in this niche. The homeowner will call when they are ready. Make the number easy to find and leave it at that.

## Speed

25. Page load under 2.5 seconds on mobile 4G. Every image lazy-loaded except the hero. No third-party scripts that block render.
