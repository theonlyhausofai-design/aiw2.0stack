# HVAC Design Vocabulary Contract

## Color System

Navy (#1B2A4A) and orange (#E86A2E) on a white (#FFFFFF) base.

Navy is the authority color. It anchors the header, footer, and primary backgrounds. It communicates stability and professionalism.

Orange is the action color. It appears only on CTAs, active states, trust chips, and highlights. It draws the eye without overwhelming. Use it sparingly. If everything is orange, nothing is.

Cool gray (#F5F7FA) alternates with white for section backgrounds. This creates visual rhythm without introducing new colors.

## Typography

One typeface: Inter. Clean, modern, highly legible at all sizes.

- Headings: Inter Bold (700). Tight line height (1.2). Navy color.
- Body: Inter Regular (400). Comfortable line height (1.6). Near-black (#1A1A1A).
- Captions and meta: Inter Regular. Slate gray (#5A6577).

No decorative fonts. No script fonts. No all-caps body text. Headings may be uppercase only in the nav or short labels.

## Layout

Grid-based. 1200px max container. Generous white space between sections (80px desktop, 48px mobile).

Cards use 8px border radius and subtle shadow (0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)). Shadow deepens on hover. No heavy borders.

Buttons use 6px border radius. Primary buttons are orange with white text. Secondary buttons are navy with white text. Ghost buttons have a navy border with navy text.

## Imagery Style

Real photography only. No illustrations, no cartoons, no clip art.

Photos should feel honest and local. Real technicians, real homes, real equipment. Avoid polished stock photography that looks staged. If the people in the photo look like models, it is the wrong photo.

Warm lighting. Clean composition. Shallow depth of field for hero images.

## Iconography

Simple line icons. Consistent stroke weight (1.5-2px). Navy or slate gray. Never filled icons with bright colors. Icons support text; they do not replace it.

## What This Design Is Not

- Not playful. No rounded bubble shapes, no pastel colors, no whimsical illustrations.
- Not dark-mode. The light background communicates openness and honesty.
- Not trendy. No glassmorphism, no gradients on cards, no neon accents. This design should look professional in five years, not dated.
- Not busy. Every element earns its space. If removing something does not hurt comprehension or conversion, remove it.

## Component Feel

| Component | Feel |
|---|---|
| Navigation | Clean, sticky, navy background, white text, orange CTA button |
| Hero | Confident, split-screen, warm photo, clear headline |
| Trust strip | Quiet authority, small badges, gray background |
| Service cards | Clean, even grid, icon + title + short description |
| Process steps | Numbered, simple icons, left-to-right on desktop, stacked on mobile |
| Reviews | Real names, star ratings, source badges, no decoration |
| Footer | Navy background, organized columns, prominent phone number |
| Forms | White card on gray background, clear labels, orange submit button |
