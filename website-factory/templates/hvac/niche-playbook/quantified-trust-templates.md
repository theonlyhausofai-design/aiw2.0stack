# HVAC Quantified Trust Statement Templates

## Purpose

These templates turn abstract claims into concrete, numbered trust statements. Replace the bracketed placeholders with real client data before publishing.

## Years and Experience

- [X]+ years serving [city/region]
- [X]+ years of HVAC experience
- Family-owned since [year]
- Serving [county/region] homeowners since [year]

## Reviews and Ratings

- [X]+ five-star reviews on Google
- [X]-star average rating across [X]+ reviews
- [X]+ five-star reviews and counting
- Rated [X] stars on Google by [city] homeowners

## Volume and Reach

- [X]+ homes served in [city/region]
- [X]+ HVAC systems installed last year
- [X]+ emergency calls answered this year
- Serving [X]+ zip codes in [county/region]

## Certifications and Credentials

- NATE-certified technicians on every call
- EPA 608 certified for safe refrigerant handling
- [Manufacturer] Factory Authorized Dealer
- BBB A+ rated since [year]
- Licensed, bonded, and insured in [state]
- All technicians background-checked and drug-tested

## Response Time

- Average response time: [X] minutes
- Same-day service on [X]% of calls
- 24/7 emergency service, 365 days a year
- A technician at your door within [X] hours, guaranteed

## Guarantees

- 100% satisfaction guaranteed or your money back
- [X]-year warranty on all installations
- [X]-year parts and labor warranty included
- No hidden fees, guaranteed in writing
- Free second opinion on any major repair

## Financial

- Financing available with payments as low as $[X]/month
- [X] months same-as-cash financing on qualifying systems
- Free estimates on all new installations
- Price match guarantee on comparable quotes

## Usage Rules

1. Never fabricate numbers. Every [X] must come from real, verifiable client data.
2. Use "+" after round numbers to avoid false precision. "500+ reviews" not "527 reviews" (unless the client wants the exact count).
3. Update numbers at least quarterly. Stale trust data erodes credibility.
4. Place the strongest trust statement in the hero trust chips. The rest distribute across the trust strip, about page, and footer.
