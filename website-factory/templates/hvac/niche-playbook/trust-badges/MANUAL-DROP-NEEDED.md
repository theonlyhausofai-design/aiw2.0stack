# Trust Badge SVGs Needed

## Instructions

Each badge listed below must be manually obtained as an SVG file and placed in this directory. Do not use rasterized logos. Do not trace or recreate trademarked logos without permission. Download official assets from each issuer's brand portal or partner resource page.

Name each file exactly as shown in the "File" column.

## Notes

- Only include badges the client actually holds. Do not display certifications or partnerships the client has not earned.
- Carrier, Trane, and Lennox are mutually exclusive in most cases. A dealer typically partners with one major manufacturer. Confirm with the client before including any manufacturer badge.
- BBB A+ requires active accreditation. Verify the client's status at bbb.org before including.
- NATE certification is per-technician, not per-company. Confirm at least one technician on staff holds current NATE certification before displaying.
- EPA 608 certification is required by law for anyone handling refrigerants. Most legitimate HVAC companies hold this. Verify anyway.
- ENERGY STAR partnership has specific enrollment requirements. Confirm with the client.

## Badge Display Specs

- Maximum height: 48px in the trust strip, 64px on the about page.
- Grayscale in the trust strip. Full color on the about page and trust section.
- Alt text for each badge: "[Badge Name] certified" or "[Badge Name] accredited."
- Link each badge to the issuer's verification page if one exists for the client.
