# Copy Deck Lessons: HVAC Niche

## Voice rules (from niche research + offer crafting)

1. Write like a trusted neighbor who knows HVAC. Not corporate. Not salesy.
2. The #1 homeowner fear is getting ripped off. Every section must reinforce transparency: upfront pricing, no hidden fees, honest diagnosis.
3. Emergency language balanced with calm authority. "We are on our way" energy, not panic.
4. Use the homeowner's own words. Pull from 02-customer-voice.md. They say "AC died", "no heat", "got three quotes and they were all different", "they never called back".
5. Name certifications specifically: "NATE-certified" not "industry-certified". "BBB A+" not "accredited".
6. Use plain numbers: "$8,500" not "competitive pricing". "200+ reviews" not "hundreds of satisfied customers".
7. Financing language matters. "$99/month" is better than "$8,500 upfront". Always mention payment options on replacement pages.

## Trust copy patterns that work for HVAC

- "[X]+ five-star reviews on Google" (specific count, not vague)
- "Same-day service. No surprise fees. Guaranteed." (triple value prop)
- "Licensed, insured, and NATE-certified." (credential stack)
- "We answer 24/7. Even at 2am on a Saturday." (availability proof)
- "Upfront pricing before we start. No surprises." (transparency)
- "If we're late, your service call is free." (guarantee with teeth)

## Copy mistakes to avoid

- Generic hero: "Welcome to [Company]" (use a trust-first or problem-first headline instead)
- Feature-first: "We offer AC repair, heating repair..." (lead with the outcome: "Your comfort, guaranteed.")
- Vague trust: "We're the best" (use specific proof: "Rated 4.9 stars by 300+ homeowners")
- Missing urgency: forgetting that HVAC emergencies need immediate language
- Missing financing: not mentioning payment options on pages about replacements ($7K-$14K purchases)

## Locked phrases (from copy-locks.json)

- Primary CTA: "Schedule Service Now"
- Form header: "Get Fast HVAC Service"
- Submit button: "Request Service"
- Privacy line: "Your information is secure and never shared."
- Mobile call label: "Tap to Call Now"
