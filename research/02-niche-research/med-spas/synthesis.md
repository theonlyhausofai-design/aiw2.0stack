# NICHE SYNTHESIS: Med Spas

**Score from Module 2A:** 24/35

---

## End customer in one sentence

A woman in her 30s-50s researching her first or next aesthetic treatment, afraid of looking unnatural or choosing the wrong provider, scrolling "Botox near me" on her phone.

## The student's client in one sentence

A local med spa doing $500K-$2M in revenue with 2-5 providers, leaking leads through missed calls and zero follow-up, sitting on a dormant patient database worth six figures in rebookings.

---

## Top 5 Insights

1. **Patient 5-year LTV ranges from $900 to $12,000.** Membership patients are worth 3x more than walk-ins. This makes reactivation campaigns extremely high-ROI. One reactivated membership patient can be worth more than the student's entire service fee.

2. **"Botox near me" gets 2.4M monthly searches, growing 500%.** Massive organic demand exists, but CPCs run $75-$150. This makes SEO and website conversion rate critical. Paid-only strategies bleed money fast.

3. **Before/after photos above the fold dramatically outperform stock photography.** This is the single most important conversion element on a med spa website. Without a strong gallery, nothing else on the page matters.

4. **Over 70% of traffic is mobile.** Booking must be possible in under 60 seconds on a phone. Sticky CTAs, minimal form fields, and click-to-call are not optional. They are the baseline.

5. **Dormant database reactivation maps directly to the Jobs Booked System.** Every med spa has hundreds or thousands of past patients who have not been contacted in months. This is the student's sharpest differentiator in this niche. No other agency in the competitive set is solving this problem.

---

## Top 3 Risks If We Pick This Niche

- **More regulatory friction than home services.** Medical advertising rules, HIPAA-adjacent requirements, before/after photo consent, and treatment claim qualifiers all add complexity. The student needs to learn these guardrails before building the first site.

- **Competitive agency landscape.** DoctorLogic, Growth99, and Plastix Marketing are established players with med-spa-specific platforms. The student is entering a market where niche agencies already exist, unlike some verticals where generalists dominate.

- **CPCs are brutal at $75-$150 per click.** If the website does not convert organically, the client's ad spend bleeds fast. The student must deliver a high-converting site or the ROI story falls apart.
