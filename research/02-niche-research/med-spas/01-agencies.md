# Med Spa Marketing Agencies

Competitive landscape scan. Eight agencies reviewed for positioning, delivery model, trust elements, and gaps.

---

## 1. Thrive Internet Marketing Agency

**URL:** thriveagency.com

**Approach:** Full-service digital marketing. SEO, PPC, social media, web design, content marketing. Generalist agency with a med spa vertical. They lead with data and case studies. Reported a +4,302% total increase in AI platform traffic for one med spa client.

**What they sell:** Results. Revenue numbers, traffic growth, lead counts. They show the math.

**Trust elements emphasized:** Case studies with hard numbers. Industry awards. Client testimonials. Long track record (20+ years).

**Pros:**
- Massive case study library with verifiable metrics
- Full-service means one vendor for everything
- Strong SEO track record across multiple verticals
- Transparent reporting dashboards

**Cons:**
- Generalist. Med spa is one of dozens of verticals
- No deep understanding of patient psychology or clinical compliance
- Cookie-cutter web design templates dressed up as custom
- Large agency overhead means slower response times

**Pricing:** Custom quotes. Estimated $1,500-$5,000/month for managed services.

**What they miss:** Patient reactivation. Dormant database mining. They focus on new patient acquisition and ignore the goldmine sitting in the existing patient list. No missed-call recovery. No speed-to-lead automation.

## 2. DoctorLogic

**URL:** doctorlogic.com

**Approach:** Healthcare-specific website and marketing platform. Proprietary CMS built for medical practices. Heavy on before/after photo management, review aggregation, and local SEO. They position as the "patient acquisition platform."

**What they sell:** A mix of features and results. They lead with the platform, then back it with patient volume claims.

**Trust elements emphasized:** HIPAA compliance. Medical-specific CMS. Before/after gallery management. Review syndication. Provider credential display.

**Pros:**
- Purpose-built for medical aesthetics
- Before/after photo management is genuinely strong
- HIPAA-aware infrastructure
- Review aggregation pulls from multiple platforms

**Cons:**
- Proprietary CMS creates vendor lock-in
- Leaving means rebuilding the entire site from scratch
- Pricing is premium for what amounts to a templated system
- Limited customization outside their platform constraints

**Pricing:** Estimated $2,000-$4,000/month platform fee plus setup.

**What they miss:** Post-visit nurture sequences. Membership program support. They get the patient in the door but do not help keep them coming back. No dormant database reactivation. No AI follow-up.

## Top 3 Patterns Winners Share

1. **Niche specificity.** The strongest agencies (DoctorLogic, Plastix, Growth99) go deep on medical aesthetics. They understand patient psychology, compliance requirements, and treatment-specific search behavior. Generalists struggle to match this depth.

2. **Before/after photo strategy.** Winners treat before/after galleries as a conversion tool, not a portfolio page. They organize by treatment, place them above the fold, and use them as the primary trust signal.

3. **Measurable results language.** Winners talk in numbers. Patient volume increases, revenue growth, cost-per-lead. They make the ROI case with data, not promises.

## Top 3 Patterns Losers Share

1. **No patient retention system.** Almost every agency stops at acquisition. They get the patient in the door and walk away. No reactivation campaigns, no membership support, no dormant database mining. This is the biggest gap in the market.

2. **Template dependence disguised as custom.** Weaker agencies use template libraries and call the output "custom design." In a visual niche like med spas, patients can tell. The sites all look the same.

3. **Missing the follow-up.** Leads come in through the site and die in the inbox. No missed-call recovery. No speed-to-lead systems. No automated nurture. The agencies build the front door but leave the hallway empty.
