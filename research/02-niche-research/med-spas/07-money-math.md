## Patient Lifetime Value (LTV)

| Patient Type | Year 1 Spend | 5-Year LTV | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-time visitor | $500-$800 | $500-$800 | No follow-up. No retention system. This is the default outcome when practices have no nurture. |
| Repeat patient (no membership) | $1,500-$3,000 | $3,000-$8,000 | Returns 2-4x per year. Tries additional treatments over time. |
| Membership patient | $2,400-$4,800/year | $8,000-$12,000 | Locked into monthly payments. Visits more frequently. Purchases add-ons. |

**Key insight:** Membership patients have 3x higher LTV than walk-ins. The difference between a $900 patient and a $12,000 patient is the follow-up system. This is where the Jobs Booked System creates massive value.

## Membership Economics

- Typical monthly membership fee: $99-$299/month
- Membership includes: 1 base treatment per month (Botox units, facial, etc.) plus 10-20% off all other services
- Average membership retention: 12-18 months
- Membership patients visit 4-6x per year (vs. 1-2x for non-members)
- Membership revenue accounts for 20-40% of total revenue at top-performing med spas
- Membership patients spend 2-3x more on add-on services during visits

**Example:** A practice with 200 members paying $149/month generates $29,800/month ($357,600/year) in predictable recurring revenue before any add-on services.

## Close Rates and Lead Requirements

| Metric | Industry Average | Top Performers |
|---|---|---|
| Website visitor to lead (form fill or call) | 3-5% | 7-10% |
| Lead to consultation booked | 40-60% | 70-80% |
| Consultation to treatment booked | 50-70% | 75-85% |
| Overall: visitor to paying patient | 0.6-2.1% | 3.7-6.8% |

**Lead math example for a $1.5M practice:**

Target: $125,000/month in revenue
Average ticket: $672
Patients needed per month: 186
At 60% close rate from consultation: 310 consultations needed
At 50% booking rate from lead: 620 leads needed
At 4% website conversion rate: 15,500 website visitors needed per month

**This is why SEO matters.** At $75-$150 CPC, buying 15,500 clicks per month would cost $1.1M-$2.3M per year. Organic traffic is the only sustainable path at these CPC levels.
