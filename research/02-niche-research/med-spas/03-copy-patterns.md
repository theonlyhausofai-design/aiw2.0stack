# Med Spa Copy Patterns

Hero headlines, CTAs, value propositions, ad hooks, and swipeable templates. All patterns drawn from top-performing med spa websites, social ads, and landing pages.

---

## Top 10 Hero Headlines

These appear above the fold on the highest-converting med spa sites.

1. "Your Best Skin Starts Here"
2. "Look Like You, Just Refreshed"
3. "Confidence Looks Good on You"
4. "Real Results. Real Patients. Real Confidence."
5. "Expert Aesthetic Care, Natural-Looking Results"
6. "The Art and Science of Looking Your Best"
7. "Subtle Results That Speak Volumes"
8. "Where Science Meets Beauty"
9. "Transform How You Feel About Your Reflection"
10. "Board-Certified Providers. Results You Can See."

**Pattern notes:** The best headlines reference transformation without promising miracles. They use "you" and "your" heavily. They avoid clinical jargon. They lean into confidence and natural outcomes over dramatic change. The word "natural" appears constantly. Transformation-focused headlines outperform welcome messages. "Your Best Skin Starts Here" beats "Welcome to [Practice Name]" every time.

## Top 10 CTAs

1. "Book Your Free Consultation"
2. "Schedule Your Treatment"
3. "See Our Before & Afters"
4. "View Our Results Gallery"
5. "Get Your Custom Treatment Plan"
6. "Claim Your New Patient Offer"
7. "Text Us to Book"
8. "Take the Skin Assessment Quiz"
9. "Download Our Treatment Guide"
10. "Join Our Membership"

**Pattern notes:** "Book" and "Schedule" dominate primary CTAs. Secondary CTAs use "See" and "View" to direct toward proof (galleries). "Free" appears in consultation CTAs but rarely elsewhere. The strongest CTAs are specific ("Book Your Botox Consultation") rather than generic ("Contact Us"). Quiz and guide CTAs work well as mid-funnel captures for patients who are not yet ready to book.

## Top 10 Value Propositions

1. Board-certified providers with specialized aesthetic training
2. Natural-looking results tailored to your unique features
3. Personalized treatment plans, not one-size-fits-all
4. Latest FDA-approved technology and products
5. Thousands of successful treatments performed
6. Allergan Diamond or Platinum provider (top 1% of injectors nationwide)
7. Complimentary consultations with no pressure to book
8. Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
9. Private, comfortable treatment environment
10. Results you can see in our gallery of real patients

**Pattern notes:** Credentials lead. "Board-certified" is the most common opener. "Natural-looking" appears in nearly every value prop list. Specificity wins. "Top 1% of injectors nationwide" is stronger than "experienced team." Social proof through volume ("thousands of treatments") beats vague quality claims.

## Common Ad Hooks (Instagram/Facebook)

These hooks drive the highest engagement on paid social for med spa accounts.

**Fear-based hooks:**
- "Worried about looking frozen? Here is how we avoid that."
- "Thinking about Botox but scared of bad results? Watch this."
- "5 red flags to look for before choosing an injector."

**Curiosity hooks:**
- "This is what $500 of Botox actually looks like."
- "Here is what nobody tells you about your first filler appointment."
- "3 treatments I recommend to every new patient (and 2 I never do)."

**Before/after hooks:**
- "6 weeks post-treatment. Swipe to see the difference."
- "Same patient. Same lighting. No filter. Just results."
- "She wanted subtle. Here is what subtle looks like."

**Educational hooks:**
- "Botox vs. Dysport: here is the actual difference."
- "When to start Botox (a board-certified answer, not a TikTok one)."
- "The real cost of cheap filler (and why we will not do it)."

**Price/offer hooks:**
- "New patient special: 20 units of Botox for $199."
- "Membership patients save 20% on every treatment. Here is how."
- "Why we post our prices online (and most med spas do not)."

## 5 Swipeable Templates

### Template 1: The Authority Hero

**Headline:** "Board-Certified. Fellowship-Trained. Thousands of Treatments Performed."
**Subhead:** "Natural-looking results from [City]'s most experienced aesthetic team."
**CTA:** "See Our Results Gallery"
**Below fold:** Before/after grid, 3 across, organized by treatment.

**When to use:** For practices with strong credentials and a deep before/after library. Leads with proof.

---

### Template 2: The Empathy-First Hero

**Headline:** "You Want to Look Like You. Just Better."
**Subhead:** "No frozen faces. No cookie-cutter plans. Just expert care built around your goals."
**CTA:** "Book Your Free Consultation"
**Below fold:** Video testimonial from a real patient describing their experience.

**When to use:** For practices targeting first-time patients. Addresses the top fear (looking unnatural) directly in the headline.

---

### Template 3: The Results-Led Hero

**Headline:** "Real Patients. Real Results. See for Yourself."
**Subhead:** "[Number] successful treatments and counting."
**CTA:** "View Before & Afters"
**Below fold:** Full-width before/after slider with treatment labels.

**When to use:** For practices with strong visual proof. Skips the pitch and goes straight to evidence.

---

### Template 4: The Offer Hero

**Headline:** "Your First Visit is On Us."
**Subhead:** "Complimentary consultation with a board-certified provider. No pressure. No obligation."
**CTA:** "Claim Your Free Consultation"
**Below fold:** Treatment menu with starting prices visible.

**When to use:** For practices in competitive markets where lowering the first-visit barrier matters. Good for new clinics building a patient base.

---

### Template 5: The Membership Hero

**Headline:** "Join [Practice Name] Insider. Save 20% on Every Treatment."

**Below fold:** Membership tier comparison table with monthly pricing.
