# HVAC SEO Landscape: Keywords, Targeting, Patterns, and Content Gaps

62% of HVAC prospects search online first. Mobile-first indexing is critical. The SEO opportunity in HVAC is massive, but the competition is real. This file maps the keyword landscape, targeting strategy, and content gaps.

---

## Primary Keyword Cluster

These are the highest-volume, highest-intent keywords. They represent homeowners ready to act.

| Keyword | Monthly Search Volume (US) | Intent | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| hvac repair near me | 30,000+ | High, emergency | Atlanta leads all metros in volume |
| ac repair near me | 25,000+ | High, emergency | Summer seasonal spike |
| hvac near me | 1,000+ | Medium, research | Broader intent, may be shopping |
| hvac companies near me | 5,000+ | Medium, research | Comparison shopping |
| ac installation near me | 3,000+ | High, purchase | High ticket, replacement intent |
| furnace repair near me | 8,000+ | High, emergency | Winter seasonal spike |
| hvac repair [city] | Varies by city | High, emergency | Local modifier is the SEO play |
| air conditioning repair near me | 12,000+ | High, emergency | Longer tail, same intent |

**Key insight:** "Near me" queries dominate HVAC search. Google interprets these as local intent and surfaces the Local Pack (map results) first. Ranking in the Local Pack is as important as ranking in organic results. Google Business Profile optimization is the foundation.

## Schema Markup (Structured Data)

Schema markup helps Google understand the page and can trigger rich results (stars, pricing, FAQ dropdowns). These are the schemas every HVAC site should implement.

### Required:
1. **LocalBusiness** (or HVACBusiness subtype): name, address, phone, hours, service area, geo coordinates
2. **AggregateRating**: star rating and review count from Google reviews
3. **Service**: individual service offerings with descriptions
4. **FAQ**: frequently asked questions on service pages and homepage

### Recommended:
5. **Review**: individual review snippets
6. **BreadcrumbList**: site navigation path
7. **Organization**: parent organization with logo and social profiles

### Implementation notes:
- Use JSON-LD format (Google's preferred method)
- Place in the `<head>` of each page
- Validate with Google's Rich Results Test before deploying
- Update the AggregateRating monthly as new reviews come in

## Content Gaps (Where Competitors Are Weak)

These are content opportunities where most HVAC websites either have thin content or no content at all. Filling these gaps creates organic traffic and builds topical authority.

### Gap 1: Cost/pricing content
Most HVAC sites avoid publishing pricing. Homeowners search for cost information constantly. The company that answers "how much does AC repair cost in Atlanta?" with a transparent, helpful page wins the click and the trust.

Target pages:
- "How Much Does AC Repair Cost in [City]?"
- "HVAC Replacement Cost: What to Expect in 2026"
- "Is a Heat Pump Worth It? Cost Breakdown for [State]"

### Gap 2: Comparison content
Homeowners compare brands, systems, and options. Most HVAC sites do not have comparison content.

Target pages:
- "Carrier vs Trane: Which HVAC System Is Right for Your Home?"
- "Heat Pump vs Central Air: Pros, Cons, and Costs"
- "2-Stage vs Variable Speed AC: What Homeowners Need to Know"

### Gap 3: Local education content
Content that addresses local climate, building codes, and energy costs performs well and is almost never produced by HVAC companies.

Target pages:
- "Best HVAC Systems for Georgia's Humidity"
- "Georgia Heat Pump Rebates in 2026: Complete Guide"
- "How Atlanta's Climate Affects Your HVAC System"

### Gap 4: Process and transparency content
Homeowners fear the unknown. Content that demystifies the HVAC process builds trust.

Target pages:
- "What to Expect During an HVAC Installation"
- "How We Size Your HVAC System (Manual J Explained)"
- "What Happens During an AC Tune-Up?"

### Gap 5: Emergency preparedness content
Seasonal, shareable content that positions the company as a helpful resource.

Target pages:
- "AC Broke? 5 Things to Check Before Calling a Technician"
- "How to Keep Your Home Cool During an AC Outage"
- "Winter Furnace Checklist for Atlanta Homeowners"
