# HVAC Money Math: Revenue, Costs, and ROI for Agency Pitch

This is the financial backbone of the HVAC niche pitch. Every number here should be usable in a sales conversation with a prospective HVAC client.

---

## Average Ticket by Job Type

| Job Type | Average Ticket | Frequency | Revenue Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Service / diagnostic call | $100 - $150 | High (daily) | Low |
| Repair (parts + labor) | $400 - $700 | Medium (weekly) | Medium |
| Maintenance / tune-up | $100 - $150 | Seasonal (spring/fall) | Low |
| Full system replacement | $7,500 - $14,000 | Low (monthly) | High |

**Blended average ticket across all job types: $1,400 - $1,800**

The blended average is useful for general ROI math, but the real pitch should focus on replacement jobs. A single replacement job at $10,000 can pay for months of marketing. Service calls and tune-ups keep the lights on. Replacements build the business.

---

## Close Rate

**Close rate on qualified leads: 38%**

This is the industry average for HVAC companies closing leads that have been qualified (meaning the lead has expressed intent, not just clicked an ad). Some companies close higher, some lower. The 38% number is conservative and defensible in a pitch.

Factors that affect close rate:
- Speed to lead (how fast the company calls back). Under 5 minutes is optimal.
- Quality of the sales process (in-home estimate vs phone quote).
- Financing availability. Offering monthly payments increases close rate on replacements by 15-25%.
- Online reviews. Companies with 4.8+ stars and 200+ reviews close higher because trust is pre-built.

---

## Leads Needed (Monthly Targets)

Here is the math for a typical HVAC client targeting replacement revenue.

**Scenario: 5 replacement jobs per month at $10,000 average**

- Revenue target: $50,000/month
- Close rate: 38%
- Leads needed: 50,000 / (10,000 x 0.38) = 14 qualified leads per month (rounded up)

**Scenario: 10 replacement jobs per month at $10,000 average**

- Revenue target: $100,000/month
- Close rate: 38%
- Leads needed: 100,000 / (10,000 x 0.38) = 27 qualified leads per month (rounded up)

**Scenario: Blended (all job types) targeting $75,000/month**

- Average ticket: $1,600 (blended)
- Close rate: 38%
- Leads needed: 75,000 / (1,600 x 0.38) = 124 leads per month (rounded up)

The blended scenario shows why most HVAC companies need volume. But the replacement-focused scenario shows why smart companies prioritize high-ticket leads. Fewer leads, more revenue, better margins.

## Cost Per Lead by Channel

| Channel | Cost Per Lead | Lead Quality | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Ads (PPC) | $104 avg | High | Immediate |
| Local Services Ads (LSA) | $25 - $55 | Very high | Immediate |
| Organic SEO | $35 - $45 | High | 3-6 months to build |
| Industry average (all channels) | $153 | Mixed | Varies |

**Key insight:** LSA is the best value channel for HVAC. Cost per lead is lowest, and lead quality is highest because the homeowner has already seen the star rating, review count, and "Google Guaranteed" badge before clicking. SEO is the best long-term investment but takes time to build. PPC is the reliable workhorse.

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Industry average CAC: $75 - $250**

CAC includes all marketing spend divided by the number of customers acquired. For an HVAC company spending $5,000/month on marketing and acquiring 25 new customers, the CAC is $200.

For replacement jobs specifically, a higher CAC is acceptable because the ticket is so large. Spending $250 to acquire a $10,000 replacement job is a 40:1 return. Spending $250 to acquire a $150 service call is not sustainable.

## ROI Sentence (Use This in the Pitch)

"If we get you 20 qualified leads a month at a 38% close rate on replacement jobs averaging $10,000, that is $76,000 in new revenue. The website pays for itself in the first week."

### Breaking that down:
- 20 leads x 38% close rate = 7.6 jobs (call it 7)
- 7 jobs x $10,000 average replacement = $70,000+ in revenue
- Website cost: $3,000 - $5,000 (one-time)
- Monthly marketing retainer: $1,500 - $3,000
- First month ROI: $70,000 revenue against $5,000 - $8,000 total cost

Even at half the projected close rate, the math works. That is the strength of a high-ticket niche.

## Payback Period Math

**Question the HVAC client is thinking: "How long until this pays for itself?"**

### Scenario A: Website only (no ongoing marketing)
- Website cost: $4,000
- Average replacement ticket: $10,000
- If the new website converts just 1 additional replacement job in the first month, the payback period is under 30 days.

### Scenario B: Website + monthly retainer
- Website cost: $4,000
- Monthly retainer: $2,000
- First month total: $6,000
- 20 leads at 38% close on $10,000 replacements = $76,000 revenue
- Payback period: Week 1

### Scenario C: Conservative (lower volume, blended tickets)
- Website cost: $4,000
- Monthly retainer: $2,000
- First month total: $6,000
- 10 leads at 38% close on blended $1,600 average = $6,080 revenue
- Payback period: Month 1 (breakeven)
- Month 2 onward: pure profit on the marketing investment

**The pitch framing:** Even the most conservative scenario breaks even in month one. The optimistic scenario (which is realistic for a well-optimized site with LSA and SEO) pays back in the first week. No other investment an HVAC company can make returns capital that fast.

## PPC Return Benchmarks

Google Ads returns 3-5x revenue per dollar spent for HVAC companies.

- Spend $1,000/month on Google Ads
- Generate $3,000 - $5,000 in attributed revenue
- That is before factoring in lifetime customer value (maintenance plans, referrals, future replacements)

Lifetime value of an HVAC customer: A homeowner who buys a system today will need maintenance annually ($100-$150/year) and a replacement in 15 years ($10,000+). The lifetime value of a single acquired customer can exceed $15,000.
