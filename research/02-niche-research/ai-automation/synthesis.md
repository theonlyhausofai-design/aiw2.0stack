The technology has outpaced the market's perception of it, creating a window where early movers can capitalize on the gap between what prospects fear and what actually exists.

## Top 3 Risks If We Pick This Niche

### 1. No vertical depth means competing with everyone.
Every GoHighLevel agency, every AI automation startup, and every tech-savvy freelancer is selling some version of "AI for businesses." Without picking a vertical (e.g., "AI for HVAC companies" or "AI for dental offices"), the student is a generalist competing on price against hundreds of other generalists. The horizontal approach offers flexibility but sacrifices positioning power.

### 2. SEO is weak. Growth depends on outbound, content, and referrals.
This niche has low search volume compared to established service categories. "AI receptionist for small business" gets 1,300 searches/month nationally. "HVAC repair near me" gets 40,000+. The student cannot rely on inbound SEO traffic to fill the pipeline. Growth requires LinkedIn content, Facebook group presence, YouTube demonstrations, and direct outreach. Juanika scored 2/5 on sales confidence in the discovery interview, which makes outbound-dependent growth a risk.

### 3. Race to the bottom on pricing.
Many GHL resellers are already offering AI voice packages at $297-$497/month with thin margins. As the tools become easier to use and more agencies enter the space, pricing pressure will intensify. The student must differentiate on service quality, vertical expertise, or bundled value (website + AI) rather than competing on the AI system alone.
