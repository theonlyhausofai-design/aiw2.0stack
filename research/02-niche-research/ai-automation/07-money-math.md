# Money Math: AI Automation for Service Businesses

Research date: 2026-06-08

---

## Core Revenue Model

The AI automation agency model runs on setup fees plus monthly recurring revenue. This is fundamentally different from a web design agency that charges a one-time project fee and then scrambles for the next client.

---

## Average Deal Size

### Setup Fees

| Package | What Is Included | Price Range |
|---|---|---|

## Client Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Average retention for AI automation services:** 12-18 months. Higher than web design (where the client leaves after the project) because the service runs continuously and the client becomes dependent on it.

**LTV calculation at $497/month:**
- 12-month retention: $5,964 recurring + $2,500 setup = $8,464
- 18-month retention: $8,946 recurring + $2,500 setup = $11,446

**LTV calculation at $997/month:**
- 12-month retention: $11,964 recurring + $4,000 setup = $15,964
- 18-month retention: $17,946 recurring + $4,000 setup = $21,946

**Why retention is high:** The AI receptionist becomes embedded in the business's operations. Turning it off means going back to missed calls, voicemail, and lost revenue. The switching cost is real and the pain of reverting is immediate.

## Leads Needed for $10,000/Month Target

### Path A: $497/month wedge offer

- Target: $10,000/month in recurring revenue
- Clients needed: 21 clients at $497/month ($10,437/month)
- Close rate assumption: 25% of qualified demos close
- Demos needed: 84 total (4-5 per week over ~18 weeks)
- Leads needed: 168 total (assuming 50% of leads book a demo)
- Timeline to target: 4-6 months of consistent outreach and content

**Plus setup fee revenue during ramp:** 21 clients x $2,500 setup = $52,500 in one-time revenue during the ramp period. This funds the business while recurring revenue builds.

### Path B: $997/month mid-range offer

- Target: $10,000/month in recurring revenue
- Clients needed: 11 clients at $997/month ($10,967/month)
- Close rate assumption: 20% of qualified demos close (higher price = lower close rate)
- Demos needed: 55 total (3-4 per week over ~16 weeks)
- Leads needed: 110 total
- Timeline to target: 3-5 months

**Plus setup fee revenue:** 11 clients x $4,000 setup = $44,000 in one-time revenue.

## The ROI Sentence

This is the sentence the student uses in every sales conversation, every ad, every landing page:

**"Your front desk misses 40% of calls. At $12.15 per missed call and 20 missed calls a week, that is $12,636 lost per year. The Jobs Booked System catches every call for $497/month. It pays for itself in the first week."**

### Breaking it down for the prospect:
- 20 missed calls/week x 52 weeks = 1,040 missed calls/year
- 1,040 x $12.15 direct cost = $12,636/year in lost revenue
- AI system cost: $497/month x 12 = $5,964/year
- Net savings: $6,672/year minimum
- ROI: 112% return on the system cost alone
- If even 10% of those missed calls convert to jobs at $800 average: 104 jobs x $800 = $83,200 in recovered revenue

The math is undeniable. This is why the problem sells itself.

## Comparison: Websites Only vs. Websites + AI Automation Bundle

### Model A: Website-Only Agency

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Average project fee | $3,000 - $7,500 |
| Monthly hosting/maintenance retainer | $100 - $300/month |
| Client LTV (12 months) | $4,200 - $11,100 |
| Clients needed for $10K/month | 33-100 clients on retainer, or 1-3 new projects/month |
| Revenue predictability | Low. Feast or famine. |
| Client stickiness | Low. Website is "done." Client has no reason to stay. |
| Upsell path | SEO, ads, redesign. All require re-selling. |
| Competitive moat | None. Every freelancer on Fiverr competes with you. |

### Model B: Website + AI Automation Bundle

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Average setup fee | $4,000 - $8,000 (website + AI system) |
| Monthly retainer | $497 - $997/month (AI receptionist + hosting) |
| Client LTV (12 months) | $9,964 - $19,964 |
| Clients needed for $10K/month | 11-20 on retainer |
| Revenue predictability | High. Recurring. Compounds monthly. |
| Client stickiness | High. They depend on the AI daily. |
| Upsell path | More automations, more workflows, more locations. Natural expansion. |
| Competitive moat | Technical. Not every web designer can build AI voice agents. |

### The delta:
- LTV increases 2-3x when you add AI automation to the bundle.
- Revenue predictability goes from "hope I close a project this month" to "I have $9,970 hitting my account on the 1st."
- The website becomes the entry point, not the product. The AI system is the product.
- Client retention doubles because the AI is embedded in daily operations.

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|

| Make.com (if used) | $0-$10/month per client |
| **Total cost of delivery per client** | **$22-$189/month** |

**Margin at $497/month:** $308-$475/month per client (62-96% gross margin)
**Margin at $997/month:** $808-$975/month per client (81-98% gross margin)

These are strong margins. The primary cost is the student's time for setup and support, not the technology.
