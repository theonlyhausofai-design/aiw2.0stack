============================================
DM PERSONALIZATION NOTES
============================================

Before sending any DM:
1. Visit their actual website on mobile. Time the load. Note what you see in the first 2 seconds.
2. Check their Google reviews: count, rating, and whether reviews appear on their site.
3. Call their business after hours. Does anyone answer? Voicemail? Nothing?
4. Check if they have online booking. Can you schedule without calling?
5. Write down 2-3 specific, real observations. Never send a generic DM.

The more specific your observation, the higher the response rate. "I noticed your site loads in 6 seconds and the phone number isn't clickable on mobile" beats "I love your work" every time.
